# Marketers & Advertisers Network (M·A·N)

> M·A·N is Ghana's peer-driven community for marketing and advertising professionals. It runs MAN Sessions (free community sessions, three a year) and the annual Digital Marketing Conference (DMC).

## Digital Marketing Conference 2026 — Built To Scale
- Date and time: Saturday 21 November 2026, 10:00–15:00 (GMT)
- Venue: British Council Accra, Ghana
- Themes: Build Yourself, Build Your Team, Build Your Work
- Format: keynote, panel discussions, partner presentations and spotlights, a pitching masterclass and breakout sessions
- Capacity: about 200 attendees
- Early bird ticket: GH₵100 — https://tickets.mojo-pay.com/events/digital-marketers-conference-2026
- Title sponsor: MojoPay

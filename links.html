Campaign link builder

Make a tracked link for DMC 2026. Visits that arrive through it show up in Google Analytics under the campaign dmc26, with the source, medium and detail you pick, so you can see which post, partner or speaker brings people in.

Ready-made partner links

One link per partner for the DMC 2026 page. Ask each partner to use theirs in every post and email.

Rules of thumb

  • Use a different detail for each post or placement, so you can compare them (li-event, li-post1).
  • Use the same link every time for the same placement, and keep details lower-case, with no spaces.
  • Visitors who decline the cookie notice are counted without their campaign details, so treat these numbers as a floor.
  • In GA4: Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition, then add the dimension Session manual ad content to see details.

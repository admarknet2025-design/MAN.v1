Isaac Newton Acquah
Co-Founder, The Innovation Spark
Austrian-Ghanaian innovation strategist who shows marketers, founders and executives how AI and digital trade can sharpen how they position themselves, sell and grow.
Full bio

Isaac Newton Acquah is an Austrian-Ghanaian innovation strategist who helps professionals and businesses turn expertise into visibility, and visibility into opportunity. He began as a Technology Scout at Siemens in Vienna and Munich, and later led business development for a recruitment-tech startup acquired by XING. He co-founded The Innovation Spark, a Ghana–Austria advisory practice that has delivered more than 20 assignments for ITC, GIZ and UNECA across over 20 African countries. As National Project Coordinator with the International Trade Centre, he led upgrading work for Ghanaian tech firms in digital marketing, service quality and AI adoption. He co-founded the Ghana Innovation Journal and co-authored Ghana’s AI Practitioners’ Handbook. He holds a BSc from the University of Warwick and an MSc from WU Vienna, and works between Accra and Vienna.

Alex has 15+ years of experience in tech, banking, telecom and upstream oil and gas, with expertise in digital solutions and marketing communications. He leads corporate affairs at GNPC Explorco and previously led digital communications and commercial innovation at AT Ghana, formerly AirtelTigo. His expertise spans AI adoption strategy, conversational AI, prompt engineering and AI-powered customer engagement. About the Venue

The British Council Accra is the flagship cultural venue for UK-Ghana partnership — centrally located, professionally equipped, and appropriate for an event of DMC 2026's calibre.

The main auditorium holds approximately 200 guests in a theatre configuration. Three breakout sessions run from 14:10 to 14:30: A, Building Your Team; B, Building Yourself; and C, Building Your Work. The hallway and frontage provide dedicated space for MojoPay's activation booth and networking during breaks.

All rooms are confirmed for DMC 2026. AV, lighting, and internet infrastructure managed by the production crew under Enoch and Justice.

Weather in November

Accra in November: warm and dry. Expect 28–32°C. Harmattan season may bring slight haze. Light clothing recommended. No rain expected.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

When and where is DMC 2026?

DMC 2026 takes place on Saturday 21 November 2026, from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, at the British Council Accra on Liberia Road, Accra, Ghana.

How much is a ticket and where do I buy it?

Early bird tickets are GH₵100 until 31 October 2026. Tickets are sold through MojoPay, the official ticketing platform, and the ticket page always shows the current price.

Who is DMC 2026 for?

DMC 2026 is for Ghana's marketing and advertising professionals. The programme is organised around three themes — Build Yourself, Build Your Team and Build Your Work — so there is something for individuals, team leads and business builders.

What is on the programme?

The day has keynotes, panel discussions, partner presentations, a pitching masterclass and three breakout sessions (14:10–14:30), across the themes Build Yourself, Build Your Team and Build Your Work. See the full schedule.

Who is speaking?

Confirmed so far: Isaac Newton Acquah, Alex Offei Lartey, Ronald Tagoe, Emelia Dede Quist, Kwaku Ofosuhene, Richard Sena Quashie, Simon Alangdee, Florentia Teye, Yvonne Dumor, Elikplim Yaa Ayegbe, Ian Hutchful and Samuel Kwabena Ansong, with Alice Mensah and Enoch “RobotBoy” Appiah Jr. as hosts. The keynote speaker will be announced. Meet the speakers.

How do I get to the venue?

The venue is on Liberia Road, Accra. Uber and Bolt are available across Accra, there is a drop-off point at the main entrance and parking is available nearby. Kotoka International Airport is about 30–45 minutes away. See the travel guide.

How can my company sponsor or partner with DMC 2026?

The Gold tier is taken by MojoPay. Silver, Bronze and Cedar tiers are still open, and Generation Ghana and FIDO are already Silver sponsors. Send an enquiry on the sponsorship page and the partnerships team will reply.

Can I tell people I'm attending?

Yes. The Make Your Flyer tool lets you upload a photo, add your name and role, and download or share a personal flyer with a ready-made caption.

What was DMC 2025 like?

DMC 2025 took place on 28 November 2025 at the Google AI Community Center in Accra, with more than 100 people in the room and 30–38 joining online. Speakers, partners and photos are in the DMC 2025 archive.

Who organises DMC?

DMC is organised by the Marketers & Advertisers Network (M·A·N), Ghana's peer-driven community for marketing and advertising professionals. M·A·N also runs free MAN Sessions through the year.

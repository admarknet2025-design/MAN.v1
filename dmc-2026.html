Build Yourself: Presentation
Isaac Newton Acquah
Co-Founder, The Innovation Spark
Austrian-Ghanaian innovation strategist who shows marketers, founders and executives how AI and digital trade can sharpen how they position themselves, sell and grow.
Full bio

Isaac Newton Acquah is an Austrian-Ghanaian innovation strategist who helps professionals and businesses turn expertise into visibility, and visibility into opportunity. He began as a Technology Scout at Siemens in Vienna and Munich, and later led business development for a recruitment-tech startup acquired by XING. He co-founded The Innovation Spark, a Ghana–Austria advisory practice that has delivered more than 20 assignments for ITC, GIZ and UNECA across over 20 African countries. As National Project Coordinator with the International Trade Centre, he led upgrading work for Ghanaian tech firms in digital marketing, service quality and AI adoption. He co-founded the Ghana Innovation Journal and co-authored Ghana’s AI Practitioners’ Handbook. He holds a BSc from the University of Warwick and an MSc from WU Vienna, and works between Accra and Vienna.

Panel · Build Yourself
Alex Offei Lartey
Marketing Communications & Digital Solutions Consultant
15+ years across tech, banking, telecom and upstream oil & gas. Leads corporate affairs at GNPC Explorco.
Full bio

Alex has 15+ years of experience in tech, banking, telecom and upstream oil and gas, with expertise in digital solutions and marketing communications. He leads corporate affairs at GNPC Explorco and previously led digital communications and commercial innovation at AT Ghana, formerly AirtelTigo. He also consults for the African Union Youth Division, Mind Builders Africa and NCVO, facilitating masterclasses on digital skills and communication for young people and startups. He was named Digital Influencer of the Year at the 2022 Ghana Information Technology & Telecom Awards, Digital Marketing Personality of the Year at the National Communications Awards, and Internal Communicator of the Year at the 2017 National PR Excellence Awards.

Panel · Build Yourself
Ronald Tagoe
Founder & CEO, mNotify · Founder, Impact AI Labs
Technology entrepreneur and AI advocate helping organisations across Africa adopt emerging technologies to improve communication, productivity and service delivery.
Full bio

Ronald Tagoe is a technology entrepreneur, Artificial Intelligence advocate and digital transformation facilitator with over a decade of experience supporting organisations across Africa to adopt emerging technologies. He is the Founder and CEO of mNotify, a leading customer engagement technology company in Ghana whose communication and automation platforms are used by businesses, NGOs and institutions to engage millions of users across Africa. He is also the Founder of Impact AI Labs, an initiative that equips entrepreneurs, professionals and organisations with practical knowledge and tools to adopt AI. His expertise spans AI adoption strategy, conversational AI, prompt engineering and AI-powered customer engagement. He holds certifications in areas including AI for Business, AI Product Management and Responsible AI from institutions such as the University of Pennsylvania, Duke University, Google Cloud, the Project Management Institute and IBM.

Panel · Build Yourself
Emelia Dede Quist
Employer Engagement Manager, Generation Ghana
PMP®-certified employer engagement and business development professional dedicated to strengthening Africa’s talent ecosystem.
Full bio

Emelia Dede Quist is a PMP®-certified Employer Engagement and Business Development professional dedicated to strengthening Africa’s talent ecosystem. At Generation Ghana, she leads strategic employer partnerships that connect skilled young people to meaningful tech-based work opportunities. Passionate about breaking longstanding patterns that limit access to employment, socio-economic mobility and representation, she champions pathways into tech and digital roles for youth, minorities and women.

About the Venue

The British Council Accra is the flagship cultural venue for UK-Ghana partnership — centrally located, professionally equipped, and appropriate for an event of DMC 2026's calibre.

The main auditorium holds approximately 200 guests in a theatre configuration. Three breakout sessions run from 14:10 to 14:30: A, Building Your Team; B, Building Yourself; and C, Building Your Work. The hallway and frontage provide dedicated space for MojoPay's activation booth and networking during breaks.

All rooms are confirmed for DMC 2026. AV, lighting, and internet infrastructure managed by the production crew under Enoch and Justice.

Travel Guide

Getting to DMC 2026

📍

Address

British Council Accra
Liberation Road, Accra
Greater Accra Region, Ghana

🚗

Getting There

  • Uber and Bolt available throughout Accra
  • Taxi from Airport: 30–45 mins
  • Parking available nearby
  • Drop-off point at main entrance
✈️

Arriving in Ghana

Kotoka International Airport (ACC) — 30–45 mins from venue. ECOWAS citizens: no visa required. Others: Ghana e-Visa available at myghanavisa.com.

🏨

Hotels Nearby

  • Labadi Beach Hotel (~15 min)
  • Movenpick Ambassador (~10 min)
  • Holiday Inn Accra Airport (~20 min)
  • Various Airbnb options in Airport Residential
💱

Money & Payments

Ghana Cedi (GHS). ATMs widely available. Mobile money (MoMo) accepted everywhere. USD accepted at hotels. MojoPay — official DMC payments partner.

🌤

Weather in November

Accra in November: warm and dry. Expect 28–32°C. Harmattan season may bring slight haze. Light clothing recommended. No rain expected.

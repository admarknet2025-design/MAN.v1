About DMC and M·A·N

Copy and paste. Short version first, then the longer one.

The Digital Marketers’ Conference (DMC) is Ghana’s annual gathering for marketing and advertising professionals, organised by the Marketers & Advertisers Network (M·A·N). DMC 2026 — Built To Scale takes place on Saturday 21 November 2026 at the British Council Accra.

The Marketers & Advertisers Network (M·A·N) is Ghana’s peer-driven community for marketing and advertising professionals. It exists to close the knowledge gap between practitioners through curated spaces where the country’s marketing and advertising people build with each other, rather than through workshops and webinars. M·A·N runs the Digital Marketers’ Conference (DMC) every year and MAN Sessions, free community sessions, three times a year. DMC 2026 — Built To Scale takes place on Saturday 21 November 2026, 10:00 to 15:00, at the British Council Accra, with keynotes, panel discussions, partner presentations, a pitching masterclass and breakout sessions around three themes: Build Yourself, Build Your Team and Build Your Work.

Story angles

  • A first-year conference that outgrew its room. DMC 2025 drew 289 registrations for a room of 100 seats, with more than 100 attending in person and 30–38 joining online. DMC 2026 moves to a venue that holds about 200.
  • Built To Scale. This year’s theme asks how Ghanaian marketers and organisations build their careers, teams and work to grow. The conference tagline: “The organisations scaling in Ghana aren’t waiting. They’re building.”
  • A community that meets all year. M·A·N runs free MAN Sessions three times a year alongside the annual conference. See the sessions.

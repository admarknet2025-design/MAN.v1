# DMC Live: operator guide (one page)

**Saturday 21 November 2026, British Council, Accra.** Three addresses on mangh.org:

| Who | Address | Sign in |
|---|---|---|
| Everyone in the room (phones) | **mangh.org/live** | none |
| Projector laptop | **mangh.org/live/screen**, press F11 for full screen | none |
| You (laptop or phone) | **mangh.org/live/control** | the team login (the same one as /studio) |

The room never needs to know the control address. The screen follows what you do within about 3 seconds; so do the phones.

## Before doors open
1. Open **/live/control**. The top bar shows how many people are connected, how many responses have come in, and how many quote cards have been made.
2. Open **/live/screen** on the projector laptop, full screen. It shows the holding slide ("Scan to join: mangh.org/live" and a QR code).
3. In **Setup**, check the sessions and times. Clock times are not locked, so you move the programme by hand. Add anything missing ("Add a session"). Type each speaker's social handle under "Speaker handles for captions".
4. Press **Holding slide** in the top bar at any time if something goes wrong. The screen goes to the join slide; phones are unaffected.
5. If you rehearsed, open **Setup → Rehearsal and reset → Reset everything** (type RESET) before doors open. Rehearsal data is marked REHEARSAL on every phone, screen and card, and Reset clears all of it (including device codes). The speaker handles and extra blocked words are kept.

## Running a session (Run tab)
1. **Session.** One tap on **Next session** moves phones and the screen to the next item. Phones show "Now" and "Next". Use "Jump to a session" if the day runs out of order.
2. **What phones are doing.** Pick one: **Cloud: Build Yourself / Build Your Team / Build Your Work**, **Ask the panel** (this session), **Build Wall**, a live **poll**, or **Nothing**. The quote-card button is always on phones, whatever you pick. Moving to the next session closes that session's Q&A (one Q&A per session).
3. **What the screen shows.** Normally **Follow phones**. Other buttons: Holding slide, **Finale: three clouds** (all three side by side), **Interview mode: top 3** (the three best questions, large), Questions list, Build Wall, **Takeaways from the room**, Poll with results, or any official quote.

## The five things people take part in
- **Word clouds.** Prompts are editable (Run tab → Word cloud prompts). One or two words, three per phone. Words the filter holds back wait under **Words → Flagged words**; release or dismiss them. Nothing flagged ever shows.
- **Ask the panel.** Questions arrive in **Questions**. Tap **Approve** or **Reject** (big buttons). Approved questions show on phones and the screen, ranked by upvotes. **Pin** keeps one at the top; **Answered** moves it down. Flagged questions say FILTER: read them before approving.
- **Polls (Polls tab).** Write the question and 2 to 5 options. Tick **Swing** to make a before/after pair: run the first, close it, run the second. The screen then shows the shift ("34% changed their minds") with both bars. For each poll: **Make live on phones**, **Show on screen**, close, delete.
- **Build Wall.** People answer "What are you building?" (120 characters, optional contact with a tick box). Approve entries in **Build Wall**; approved ones scroll on the screen. **Download wall CSV** has opted-in contacts for the What I Built cards.
- **Quote cards.** See below. Attendees make these on their own phones.

## Quote cards: who publishes what
There are two kinds of card, and they are never mixed up.

**Official quote** (a line a speaker actually said)

- The **note-taker** (or you) writes it in **Official quotes**: pick the session, pick the speaker from the programme (or type a name), type the line (up to 220 characters, as close to verbatim as you can), press **Save quote**, then **Publish to phones**.
- It appears on phones under "Quotes from this session" and in the card maker, with the speaker's name, title and photo. It stays on that session.
- **Sponsor rule.** If the speaker represents a sponsor or partner (marked in the programme data: for example MojoPay, mNotify, Generation Ghana, or ticked by hand), **Publish to phones is blocked** until you tick **Sponsor-approved**. Tick it only after the sponsor has agreed the exact words. If you edit the words afterwards, the tick is cleared and the quote is withdrawn until you approve it again.
- You can also put an official quote on the screen: Run → "Put an official quote on the screen…".

**My takeaway** (an attendee's own words)

- Typed by the attendee (up to 140 characters). The card says **"MY TAKEAWAY"** and **"— Ama, at DMC 2026"**. The session and its speakers appear only as context ("From: Build Your Team · Discussion with …"). A sponsor's representative is never named on one.
- Every takeaway card made goes to **Takeaways** with a small preview. Nothing reaches the screen unless you tap **Send to screen**; then it shows as "Takeaways from the room", first name only.

Cards are drawn on the phone. The card maker works with no signal once it has opened. Words the filter dislikes cannot be put on a card.

## If something goes wrong
- **Screen shows the wrong thing:** Holding slide, then pick again.
- **Rude or off-topic content:** it is held for you; nothing reaches a screen without your approval (word clouds show only what passed the filter, and you can add blocked words under **Words**).
- **Phones say "Reconnecting…":** they keep working offline and send when the signal returns. Nothing is lost.
- **Opened the wrong activity:** press **Nothing**, then the right one. Closing and re-opening an activity does not delete what people sent.
- **After the event:** **Words → Export (CSV)** downloads everything (wall, questions, words, polls, takeaways, quotes, card analytics). Phones' device codes are deleted 30 days after the event.

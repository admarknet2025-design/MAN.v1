Privacy and Terms

Last updated 17 October 2026. DMC 2026 is hosted by the Marketers & Advertisers Network (MAN), Accra, Ghana. In this page, "we" means MAN.

PrivacyTerms

Privacy

This covers the information you give us through the DMC 2026 sponsorship form.

What we collect

Your company name, website (if given), your name, role (if given), email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the sponsorship tier you pick, and the areas of the event you are interested in.

Why we collect it

To reply to your sponsorship enquiry, send you the sponsor pack, and talk with you about DMC 2026. We do not use it for anything else.

Who sees it

Only the MAN team working on DMC 2026 sponsorships and partnerships. We do not sell your information or share it with other companies for their marketing.

How long we keep it

For up to 12 months after DMC 2026, then we delete it. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will.

Joining MAN

If you choose to join the Marketers & Advertisers Network, we use the details you give us to set up your membership, add you to our community channels, and tell you about MAN events and opportunities. Submitting the sponsorship form does not make you a member. You can leave MAN at any time, and ask us to delete your details, by emailing us.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email us and we will respond.

Terms

  • Submitting the form tells us you are interested. It is not an agreement and it does not reserve a tier, slot or activation.
  • Any sponsorship is only agreed in a separate written agreement signed by you and MAN. Prices, benefits and availability are set out there.
  • We may decline or defer any enquiry.
  • Please give accurate information and only submit details you are allowed to share, including a colleague's contact details.
  • We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of Ghana.

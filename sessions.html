MAN Sessions are the heartbeat of the MAN community — Q1, Q2, and Q3. Where DMC is the annual gathering, Sessions are the ongoing conversation — smaller, more intimate, and built for the kind of depth you can't get in a conference room with 200 people.

Each session features one practitioner in a fireside format. No slides. No polished talks. Just real conversation about what's working, what failed, and what they're building next.

Sessions are open to MAN community members. Join the network to access registration for upcoming sessions.

How to Attend

Three steps to the room.

01

Join the MAN Network

MAN Sessions are open to MAN community members. Apply via the form on the home page — the team reviews every application.

Apply to Join MAN →
02

Register for Each Session

Each quarter, registration opens to community members. Spaces are limited — register early. Virtual sessions use a link shared to registered attendees; in-person sessions are at a confirmed Accra venue.

03

Show Up and Participate

Sessions run 90 minutes to 2 hours. Come with questions. The format is fireside — conversational, not lecture. Your participation is the point of the room.

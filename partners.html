Sponsorship tiers

Tier structure

2026 structure. 2027 packages to be confirmed.

Gold / Headline

Everything in Silver, plus “Presented by” headline status, a 15-minute product demonstration and dominant brand presence. One per conference.

Silver

Everything in Bronze, plus a 10-minute speaking slot, an exhibition booth, and a session or segment aligned with your brand.

Bronze

Everything in Cedar, plus visibility across key event assets, a panel session of your choice, and your logo in attendee communications.

Cedar

A souvenir pack, your logo on official flyers and social media, and a 5-minute speaking opportunity. Entry-level presence.

DMC 2026 is not a mass-market event. It is a deliberately limited gathering of Ghana's most serious marketing and advertising practitioners — people who are actively building brands, campaigns, and careers. They are not easy to reach. They are in this room.

A sponsorship of the Digital Marketers’ Conference is a presence decision, not a coverage decision. Every package puts your organisation in front of a curated audience with a high signal-to-noise ratio — not a stadium of people scrolling past your logo.

Four tiers. Gold is taken by MojoPay, Silver already has two sponsors (Generation Ghana and FIDO) with room for more, and Bronze and Cedar are open. Contact Alice to discuss which is right for your organisation.

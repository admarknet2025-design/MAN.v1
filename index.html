About MAN

Ghana's peer-driven community for marketing and advertising professionals.

Marketers & Advertisers Network exists to close the knowledge gap between practitioners. Not workshops. Not webinars. Curated spaces where Ghana's best marketing and advertising minds build with each other.

We run the Digital Marketers’ Conference annually and MAN Sessions three times a year (Q1, Q2, Q3) — both designed to be worth the room, not just worth attending.

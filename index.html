About the Venue

The British Council Accra is the flagship cultural venue for UK-Ghana partnership — centrally located, professionally equipped, and appropriate for an event of DMC 2026's calibre.

The main auditorium holds approximately 200 guests in a theatre configuration. The Executive Suite is a separate, smaller room used for the Workshop breakout. The hallway and frontage provide dedicated space for MojoPay's activation booth and networking during breaks.

All rooms are confirmed for DMC 2026. AV, lighting, and internet infrastructure managed by the production crew under Enock and Justice.

Travel Guide

Getting to DMC 2026

📍

Address

British Council Accra
Liberation Road, Accra
Greater Accra Region, Ghana

🚗

Getting There

  • Uber and Bolt available throughout Accra
  • Taxi from Airport: 30–45 mins
  • Parking available nearby
  • Drop-off point at main entrance
✈️

Arriving in Ghana

Kotoka International Airport (ACC) — 30–45 mins from venue. ECOWAS citizens: no visa required. Others: Ghana e-Visa available at myghanavisa.com.

🏨

Hotels Nearby

  • Labadi Beach Hotel (~15 min)
  • Movenpick Ambassador (~10 min)
  • Holiday Inn Accra Airport (~20 min)
  • Various Airbnb options in Airport Residential
💱

Money & Payments

Ghana Cedi (GHS). ATMs widely available. Mobile money (MoMo) accepted everywhere. USD accepted at hotels. MojoPay — official DMC payments partner.

🌤

Weather in November

Accra in November: warm and dry. Expect 28–32°C. Harmattan season may bring slight haze. Light clothing recommended. No rain expected.

MAN Sessions are the quarterly heartbeat of the MAN community. Where DMC is the annual gathering, Sessions are the ongoing conversation — smaller, more intimate, and built for the kind of depth you can't get in a conference room with 200 people.

Each session features one practitioner in a fireside format. No slides. No polished talks. Just real conversation about what's working, what failed, and what they're building next.

Sessions are open to MAN community members. Join the network to access registration for upcoming sessions.

DMC 2025 · Google as Title Sponsor & Venue Partner

DMC 2025 was held at the Google AI Community Center in Accra on 28 November 2025. Google served as title sponsor and venue partner, providing the space and platform support for the AI in Marketing panel discussion.

The day brought together 100+ physical attendees and 30–38 online participants, with 289 total registrations — a significant oversubscription that validated the format and set a new baseline for DMC 2026.

"The interest in DMC 2025 exceeded our expectations. 289 registrations for a room with 100 seats tells us the community needs more than one day a year."

Three panels, four parallel breakout sessions, and a keynote from Stephen Boadi. The first DMC — and the proof of concept that led directly to DMC 2026: Built To Scale at the British Council.

DMC 2025 was the first DMC — and first proofs of concept rarely go perfectly. The community showed up in numbers far beyond the room's capacity. The wifi failed. Generation Ghana's $1,000 sponsorship didn't arrive by event day, forcing emergency founder contributions.

"No website existed. We relied entirely on social media, WhatsApp, and email."

The lessons from 2025 are now built into DMC 2026. A proper venue with professional AV. A dedicated website. MojoPay as title sponsor with a formal agreement. And the British Council — a room that can hold the room DMC 2025 proved exists.

DMC 2025 wasn't perfect. That's what made DMC 2026 possible.
